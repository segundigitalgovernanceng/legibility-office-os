# Legibility Office AI Governance Framework (AIGF)

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Governance Framework (AIGF) provides an institutional architecture for governing Artificial Intelligence within governments, regulators, development finance institutions, and public organizations.

Rather than treating AI as a standalone technology, the framework positions AI as an institutional capability embedded within the Institutional Operating System (IOS).

Its objective is not simply responsible AI, but institutionally aligned AI.

---

# Core Principle

AI should strengthen institutions.

Institutions should govern AI.

Neither should replace the other.

---

# Definition

AI Governance is the institutional capability to design, deploy, oversee, regulate, and continuously improve AI systems in ways that strengthen public trust, accountability, and long-term institutional capability.

---

# AI Governance Objectives

AI should

- Improve public decision making
- Strengthen institutional capability
- Reduce administrative burden
- Increase transparency
- Improve coordination
- Preserve accountability
- Enhance public value
