# Legibility Office Procurement for AI Framework (PAIF)

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Procurement for AI Framework (PAIF) provides an institutional architecture for acquiring, deploying, managing, and governing Artificial Intelligence within public institutions.

The framework recognizes that governments do not simply procure AI software.

They procure institutional capability.

Poor AI procurement creates technological dependence.

Strategic AI procurement creates institutional resilience.

---

# Core Principle

Governments should procure AI capability, not AI products.

The objective is institutional capability accumulation rather than technology acquisition.
