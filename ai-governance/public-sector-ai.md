## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Public Sector AI Framework (PSAF) provides a governance architecture for designing, deploying, governing, and scaling Artificial Intelligence across government institutions.

Unlike commercial AI adoption, public sector AI exists to improve governance, strengthen institutional capability, increase public value, and enhance democratic accountability.

AI is therefore treated as public institutional infrastructure.

---

# Core Principle

Public sector AI should strengthen institutions.

Institutions should remain accountable for AI.

Technology serves governance.

Governance never serves technology.

---

# Definition

Public Sector AI is the application of Artificial Intelligence to government functions in ways that improve public administration while preserving legality, accountability, transparency, inclusion, and public trust.
