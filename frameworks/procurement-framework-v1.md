# Legibility Office Procurement Framework v1.0

## Procurement as the Institutional Execution Engine

**Version:** 1.0

**Project:** Legibility Office Institutional Operating System (IOS)

---

# Executive Summary

Procurement is often treated as an administrative function.

Within the Institutional Operating System (IOS), procurement is understood differently.

Procurement is the institutional mechanism through which governments transform public resources into national capability.

Every bridge, hospital, school, digital identity system, AI platform, power plant, fibre network, and public service ultimately passes through procurement.

Procurement therefore determines not only what governments buy, but how institutions execute national strategy.

The quality of procurement becomes the quality of state capability.

---

# Definition

**Institutional Procurement** is the strategic governance function that converts public resources into long-term institutional capability through transparent, accountable, competitive, and coordinated acquisition systems.

Procurement is execution architecture.

---

# Core Principle

> Procurement is not about purchasing.

> Procurement is how governments build capability.

Legibility Office views procurement as one of the most underestimated determinants of national competitiveness.
