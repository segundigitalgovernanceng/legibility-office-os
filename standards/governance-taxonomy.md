# Purpose

The Legibility Office Governance Taxonomy (LGT) establishes a common language for understanding governance across institutions, governments, Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), Artificial Intelligence, procurement, and public administration.

Rather than treating governance as a single concept, the taxonomy decomposes governance into structured institutional components that can be analyzed, measured, compared, and improved.

The taxonomy serves as the semantic foundation of the Institutional Operating System (IOS).

---

# Core Principle

Institutions cannot coordinate without a shared governance vocabulary.

Classification precedes coordination.

---

# Governance Definition

Governance is the institutional capability to establish authority, coordinate action, allocate resources, manage risk, preserve accountability, and continuously improve collective decision-making.
