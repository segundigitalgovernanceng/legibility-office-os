## Version

1.0

# Purpose

The Institutional Standards Framework (ISF) defines the common standards that enable governments, institutions, Digital Public Infrastructure, procurement systems, and AI-enabled public administration to operate as one coherent Institutional Operating System (IOS).

Standards are the invisible architecture that allows independent institutions to coordinate, scale, and learn.

Without standards, institutions fragment.

With standards, institutions compound capability.

---

# Core Principle

Institutions coordinate through standards.

Capability scales through standardization.

---

# Definition

Institutional standards are the shared rules, models, protocols, definitions, and governance mechanisms that enable institutions to exchange information, execute consistently, preserve accountability, and create public value.
