# Legibility Office Procurement Standards Framework (PSF)

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Procurement Standards Framework (PSF) establishes the institutional standards required for procurement systems to operate consistently, transparently, interoperably, and strategically across government.

Within the Institutional Operating System (IOS), procurement standards transform procurement from isolated purchasing activities into a coordinated institutional capability.

---

# Core Principle

Procurement capability scales through standardization.

Standards reduce uncertainty, strengthen accountability, improve interoperability, and preserve institutional memory.

---

# Definition

Procurement standards are the common rules, specifications, governance mechanisms, documentation requirements, and technical protocols that enable public procurement systems to function consistently across institutions.
