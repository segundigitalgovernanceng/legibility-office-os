Legibility Office OS Code of Conduct
Our Purpose

Legibility Office OS exists to make institutions more understandable, more accountable, and more capable.

We believe open collaboration strengthens governance, procurement, Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), and institutional capability worldwide.

Every interaction within this project should contribute to that mission.

Our Values

We are committed to building a community based on:

Respect
Intellectual honesty
Systems thinking
Evidence-based discussion
Professional integrity
Institutional responsibility
Openness to constructive criticism
Long-term public value
Expected Behavior

Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

Treat others with professionalism and respect.
Assume good faith during technical and policy discussions.
Support claims with evidence wherever possible.
Welcome diverse perspectives and constructive disagreement.
Focus criticism on ideas, frameworks, and evidence—not individuals.
Share knowledge generously.
Help improve the quality and clarity of the project.
Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

Personal attacks or insults.
Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
Intimidation or abusive language.
Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
Misrepresentation of evidence or deliberate misinformation.
Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or commercial solicitation.
Publishing confidential or proprietary information without permission.
Plagiarism or failure to attribute sources appropriately.
Professional Disagreement

Legibility Office OS encourages rigorous debate.

Institutional design often involves competing ideas and perspectives.

Disagreement is welcome when it is:

Respectful
Evidence-based
Constructive
Focused on improving the work

The goal is not to win arguments.

The goal is to build better institutional knowledge.

Evidence Standards

Contributors should distinguish clearly between:

Evidence
Interpretation
Opinion
Speculation

Where possible:

Cite sources.
Explain assumptions.
Acknowledge uncertainty.

Transparency builds trust.

Inclusive Community

We welcome contributors regardless of:

Nationality
Profession
Academic background
Government affiliation
Industry
Gender
Ethnicity
Religion
Political views

Institutional intelligence benefits from diverse perspectives.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

GitHub Issues
Pull Requests
Discussions
Documentation
Research contributions
Diagrams
Templates
Community events
Project communications
Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

Maintaining a respectful environment.
Reviewing contributions fairly.
Explaining decisions transparently.
Enforcing this Code of Conduct consistently.
Protecting contributors from harassment or abuse.
Enforcement

If behavior violates this Code of Conduct, maintainers may take actions including:

Private warning.
Request for corrective action.
Removal of inappropriate content.
Temporary suspension from participation.
Permanent removal from the project.

Enforcement decisions will prioritize protecting the integrity of the community.

Our Long-Term Commitment

Legibility Office OS is more than an open-source repository.

It is an effort to build a global community dedicated to improving institutional capability through shared knowledge, practical frameworks, and responsible collaboration.

We believe that better institutions begin with better conversations.

Thank you for helping build them.
