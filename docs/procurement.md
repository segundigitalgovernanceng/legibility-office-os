# Procurement Framework

## Procurement as the Capability Engine of the Institutional Operating System

**Version:** 0.1

**Project:** Legibility Office Institutional Operating System (IOS)

---

# Executive Summary

Procurement is often viewed as an administrative process for purchasing goods and services.

This perspective is incomplete.

Within the Institutional Operating System (IOS), procurement is the **primary mechanism through which strategy becomes execution and public resources become institutional capability.**

Every national ambition eventually passes through procurement.

Industrial policy.

Digital Public Infrastructure.

Artificial Intelligence.

Healthcare.

Education.

Infrastructure.

Energy.

Innovation.

If governance determines **what** institutions seek to achieve, procurement determines **whether they can achieve it.**

Procurement is therefore not a back-office function.

It is a strategic capability.

---

# Definition

**Strategic procurement** is the institutional capability that allocates public resources in ways that continuously strengthen national capability, institutional performance, market development, and public value.

Procurement is not simply about purchasing.

It is about capability allocation.
