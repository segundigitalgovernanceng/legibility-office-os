# Legibility Office Procurement Risk Framework (PRF)

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

The Procurement Risk Framework (PRF) provides a structured approach to identifying, assessing, managing, and continuously monitoring procurement risks within public institutions.

Rather than viewing procurement risk solely as fraud or compliance failure, the framework treats risk as any condition that weakens institutional capability, governance, public value, or long-term national competitiveness.

---

# Core Principle

Procurement risk is institutional risk.

Every procurement failure weakens institutional capability.
