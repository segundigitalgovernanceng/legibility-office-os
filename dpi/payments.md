# Purpose

Payments are a foundational governance primitive within the Institutional Operating System (IOS).

While identity establishes who participates in an institution, payments determine how value moves through it.

Every government, market, and institution ultimately coordinates resources through payment systems.

Payments are therefore institutional infrastructure rather than merely financial infrastructure.

---

# Core Principle

Institutions allocate authority through governance.

They allocate value through payments.

Without trusted payment systems, institutional capability cannot scale.
