## Version

1.0

# Purpose

Identity is the foundational governance primitive of every Institutional Operating System (IOS).

Institutions cannot govern people, assets, services, or transactions unless they can reliably identify them.

Identity is therefore not simply a technology.

It is institutional infrastructure.

---

# Core Principle

No governance exists without identity.

Every institutional capability depends on the ability to know who, what, where, and under what authority an action occurs.

---
