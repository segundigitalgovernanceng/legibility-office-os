## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

Interoperability is the institutional capability that enables independent systems, organizations, and governments to work together without losing autonomy.

Within the Institutional Operating System (IOS), interoperability transforms isolated institutional capabilities into coordinated national capability.

Interoperability is therefore governance infrastructure.

---

# Core Principle

Institutions become capable individually.

States become capable collectively.

Interoperability is the mechanism that enables collective institutional capability.

---

# Definition

Interoperability is the ability of institutions, systems, people, policies, and technologies to exchange information, coordinate decisions, and deliver services through shared standards while preserving institutional independence.
